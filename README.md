# Dan Mardis

I’m an IT professional who enjoys solving messy problems: disconnected systems,
manual work, unreliable data, and questions that take real digging to answer.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of business systems, automation,
integrations, data, and research. I build tools and workflows, connect systems
through APIs, track down root causes, and turn scattered information into
something people can actually use.

## What I work on

- Business systems, internal tools, and technical operations
- API, identity, HRIS, and SaaS integrations
- Workflow automation and process improvement
- Data quality, reconciliation, and reporting
- Technical and historical research
- Python, SQL, PySpark, Microsoft Fabric, and Power BI
- Documentation, reliability, and disaster recovery

## How I work

I like to understand how the whole process actually works before changing it.
That means talking to the people who use it, checking the source data, tracing
failures upstream, and documenting what I find. I care more about fixing the
underlying problem than adding another dashboard or tool.

I use AI-assisted development tools where they are useful, but I make the
decisions about requirements, architecture, security, testing, and release.

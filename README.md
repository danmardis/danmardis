# Dan Mardis

I am an advanced researcher, business-systems builder, and automation-focused
problem solver. I turn fragmented information, disconnected tools, and manual
processes into clearer evidence, better workflows, and reliable solutions.

My work sits at the intersection of research, integrations, process improvement,
automation, and data. I enjoy tracing difficult questions across primary sources,
connecting systems through APIs, streamlining repetitive work, and designing
creative solutions that are practical enough to operate and maintain.

## What I work on

- Advanced research, source discovery, provenance, and evidence synthesis
- API, identity, HRIS, and SaaS integrations
- Workflow automation and end-to-end process improvement
- Data-quality auditing and cross-system reconciliation
- Internal tools and creative operational solutions
- Python, SQL, PySpark, Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and related platforms
- Privacy-conscious, testable system design
- Operational reliability and disaster-recovery planning

## How I approach the work

I start by understanding the real problem, the available evidence, the system
boundaries, and the points where work is being lost, repeated, delayed, or made
needlessly difficult.

I favor explainable requirements, maintainable automation, thoughtful
integrations, honest tradeoffs, and solutions that improve the process rather
than merely adding another layer of software.

I use AI-assisted development tools where they improve velocity, while retaining
ownership of research direction, requirements, architecture, acceptance
criteria, validation strategy, security boundaries, and release decisions.

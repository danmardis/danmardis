# Dan Mardis

I am an IT professional, historical researcher, business-systems builder, and
automation-focused problem solver. I enjoy difficult problems where information
is fragmented, systems are disconnected, and important context has been lost
over time.

My work spans IT, historical and technical research, integrations, process
improvement, automation, and data. I trace hard-to-find evidence across primary
sources, connect systems through APIs, eliminate repetitive work, and design
creative solutions that are practical enough to operate, support, and improve.

## What I work on

- IT systems, technical operations, and internal platforms
- Historical research, source discovery, provenance, and evidence synthesis
- Lost technical knowledge recovery and systems archaeology
- API, identity, HRIS, and SaaS integrations
- Workflow automation and end-to-end process improvement
- Data-quality auditing and cross-system reconciliation
- Internal tools and creative operational solutions
- Python, SQL, PySpark, Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, and related platforms
- Privacy-conscious, testable system design
- Operational reliability and disaster-recovery planning

## How I approach the work

I start by understanding the real problem, the available evidence, the system
boundaries, and the points where work is being lost, repeated, delayed, or made
needlessly difficult.

I favor primary sources, explicit provenance, explainable requirements,
maintainable automation, thoughtful integrations, honest tradeoffs, and
solutions that improve the process rather than merely adding another layer of
software.

I use AI-assisted development tools where they improve velocity, while retaining
ownership of research direction, requirements, architecture, acceptance
criteria, validation strategy, security boundaries, and release decisions.
